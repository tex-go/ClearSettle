  ClearSettle is an eCommerce Settlement Reconciliation SaaS platform for
  Indian multi-platform sellers (focus: Tirupur textile industry).

  It provides automated reconciliation, dispute recovery, GST compliance,
  and seller-prospecting tools across Amazon, Flipkart, and Meesho.

  Repository:   https://github.com/tex-go/ClearSettle
  Release Tag:  v1.0.0
